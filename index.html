What the risk classes mean
RsI — highest risk. May partially or fully collapse in a major earthquake. This is a genuine life-safety danger.
RsII — high risk. Likely to suffer major structural damage in a major earthquake, but is unlikely to collapse outright.
RsIII — moderate risk. May show structural damage in a major earthquake, but it isn't expected to seriously threaten safety.
RsIV — lowest classified risk. Expected to hold up in an earthquake about as well as a modern, code-compliant building.
